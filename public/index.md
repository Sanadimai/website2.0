## The problem

A seven-second Arabic voice note arrives at 9:47 PM. Nobody hears it until morning. The patient books the Dubai clinic five minutes away.

Your receptionist is excellent — for eight hours. The other sixteen, and the thirty simultaneous messages while she is checking someone in, are where bookings quietly leak.

**Illustrative math** (a worked example, not a measured result): average consultation AED 400 × 3 convertible messages missed per day × ~21 days ≈ **AED 25,000** left unrecovered monthly.

## What Sanad handles

1. **Voice notes, understood** — Gulf Arabic, MSA and English transcribed in memory; raw audio is never stored.
2. **Dental & aesthetics booking** — confirmed in your calendar without forms, links or another app.
3. **Reminders & no-show recovery** — approved WhatsApp templates; opt-outs always respected.
4. **FAQs from your clinic's own facts** — hours, location, price ranges, insurance; never invented.
5. **Instagram & social leads** — instant reply, same booking flow (Growth plan).
6. **Instant human handoff** — a patient typing "human" or «أريد التحدث مع شخص» stops the bot immediately.

## Honest numbers

Every booking is tagged the moment it happens:

- `bot-originated` — captured by Sanad alone, after hours, no human touch. The only number in the headline report.
- `bot-assisted` — Sanad helped, staff finished. Reported separately, never merged upward.
- `staff` — your team's own bookings. Excluded by design; ambiguous cases default downward.

> "If a patient would have booked anyway, it is not on your report. A smaller number — but one you can make a renewal decision on."

## Security & compliance

- Patient data stays in the UAE — every stateful component runs on in-country infrastructure by architecture.
- A PDPL-aligned data-processing agreement, signed: scope limited to scheduling data, named sub-processors, 72-hour breach notice, deletion on exit.
- **Never medical advice.** Every clinical question gets one fixed reply: "For medical questions, the doctor will discuss this during your appointment."
- A security whitepaper authored and signed by the co-founder responsible for security.
