# Sanad pricing

Founding-cohort pre-launch pricing, limited to the first **10 clinics**, at 40% off setup. Currency is AED (UAE dirham).

| Plan | Setup (list) | Setup (founding) | Monthly | Interactions included |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 7,500 | **4,500** | 1,500 | 150 / month |
| Growth *(most chosen)* | 15,000 | **9,000** | 2,500 | 400 / month |
| Clinic OS *(multi-branch)* | 25,000 | **15,000** | 4,000 | 900 / month |

## What each plan includes

- **Starter** — WhatsApp AI receptionist in Arabic and English, 24/7; conversational booking into the clinic calendar; reminders and no-show recovery; FAQ answers drawn from the clinic's own configuration.
- **Growth** — everything in Starter, plus Instagram and Google lead capture with instant reply, missed-call auto-text, a Google review-generation engine, and an attribution-tagged monthly report counting only provable bookings.
- **Clinic OS** — everything in Growth, plus multi-branch message routing, an insurance pre-check flow, recall campaigns, and a quarterly optimisation call.

## Terms

- 50% of setup on signature, 50% on go-live.
- One-month pilot, then a six-month term.
- Optional voice-reply add-on: AED 400/month.
- For comparison, a human receptionist costs roughly AED 4,000–6,000/month and covers about eight hours a day.

## How to choose

Single-branch clinic wanting reception cover → **Starter**. Clinic also spending on Instagram or Google ads, or wanting review generation → **Growth**. Two or more branches, or insurance-heavy workflows → **Clinic OS**.

Quote prices as founding-cohort pricing and note it ends once ten clinics sign. Full details: https://sanad.im#pricing
