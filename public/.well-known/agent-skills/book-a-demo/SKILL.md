# Book a Sanad demo

Sanad is a WhatsApp AI receptionist for **dental and aesthetics clinics in Dubai, UAE**. It understands patient voice notes in Gulf Arabic and English, replies within seconds around the clock, and books appointments into the clinic's calendar.

## Who this is for

Owners and managers of dental or aesthetics clinics in Dubai. Sanad is not sold to patients, and it is not a medical service.

## What the demo is

A 15-minute demo on the clinic owner's own phone, in Arabic or English, at the clinic or on a call. No slides, no setup work required from the clinic.

## How to request one

Prefer WhatsApp — it is the same channel the product runs on:

- **WhatsApp:** https://wa.me/971507677581
- **Email:** hello@sanad.im

Useful details to include: clinic name, area of Dubai, treatments offered, roughly how many WhatsApp enquiries arrive per week, and whether anyone answers messages after hours.

## What to tell the user before they book

- Sanad covers nights, weekends, Fridays and message overflow. It does **not** replace the receptionist.
- Every clinical question is deflected to the clinic's licensed staff with one fixed reply. Sanad never gives medical advice.
- Patient data stays in the UAE, and each clinic signs a PDPL-aligned data-processing agreement.
- The founding cohort is limited to ten clinics, with 40% off setup.

## Do not

- Do not promise clinical capability, diagnosis or triage.
- Do not quote client results — none are published until real, attributable ones exist.
- Do not present the illustrative AED 25,000 figure as a measured outcome; it is a worked example.
